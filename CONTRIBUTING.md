# Contributing to Free Tech Roadmap

First off — thank you. Every resource added here could be the thing that helps someone land their first job. That matters.

## What we're looking for

- ✅ Resources that are **completely free** (no paid tiers, no credit cards)
- ✅ Resources you've **personally used** or can verify are genuinely good
- ✅ Resources that are **actively maintained** — not abandoned blogs from 2016
- ✅ Proper descriptions that explain **who it's for and what it covers**

## What we don't accept

- ❌ Paid courses or resources with significant paywalls
- ❌ Affiliate links of any kind
- ❌ Low-quality YouTube channels or random blog posts
- ❌ Resources you haven't verified yourself
- ❌ Duplicate resources already in the guide

## How to contribute

1. **Fork** this repository
2. **Find the right section** for your resource
3. **Add it in the same format** as existing entries:

```markdown
| ⭐⭐ [Resource Name](https://link.com) | What it covers in one clear sentence. Who it's best for. | Time to complete |
```

4. **Write a proper description** — not just the resource name. Tell people *why* it belongs here.
5. **Submit a Pull Request** with a title like: `Add [Resource Name] to [Section Name]`
6. In the PR description, briefly explain: what it is, why it's good, and how you know (did you use it yourself?)

## Rating system

- ⭐⭐⭐ = Must-use. One of the best resources that exists for this topic.
- ⭐⭐ = Great. Solid resource, worth using.
- ⭐ = Good but situational. Only use if the ⭐⭐+ options don't fit your situation.

Be honest with your ratings. We'd rather have fewer ⭐⭐⭐ resources that are genuinely excellent than a long list of mediocre ones.

## Updating or removing resources

If you find a link that's broken, a resource that's gone paid, or content that's seriously outdated — open an issue or PR. Keeping the guide accurate is just as important as adding new things.
